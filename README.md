## AI models and decision systems

- Google announced Gemini Decision 1, a calibrated multimodal decision model that takes up to 1000 questions at $0.10 per million input tokens with about 200ms end-to-end latency, beating Anthropic to a production decision model <sup>[1](<https://latent.space/p/ainews-d-day-decision-models-day/>)</sup>.
- The "system one" trend, non-generative models returning structured choices, scores or probabilities that are faster and cheaper than reasoning LLMs, was the main theme of the AI Engineer Code Summit <sup>[2](<https://latent.space/p/jev-revolution/>)</sup>.
- Jev, the product that kicked off this trend, is pitched by its CEO as "reliable intelligence" for automating tasks, with reliability as the core motivation <sup>[2](<https://latent.space/p/jev-revolution/>)</sup>.

## Developer tools and local analytics

- DuckDB's WebAssembly build lets an app run analytical queries in the browser without a database server; the example app stores uploaded datasets as Parquet files in S3 and loads them into DuckDB when a project opens <sup>[3](<https://duckdb.org/2026/11/12/local-analytics-without-a-database-server.html>)</sup>.
- The same write-up argues for keeping data local by default and syncing via the cloud only when the user chooses <sup>[3](<https://duckdb.org/2026/11/12/local-analytics-without-a-database-server.html>)</sup>.

## Business and customer applications

- Holiday Extras reports using Codex to turn customer expertise into app improvements and higher booking conversion <sup>[4](<https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/holidays-extras-codex>)</sup>.
- Gilbert + Tobin describes how it is rethinking the business of law with AI, in a conversation with its CEO <sup>[5](<https://openai.com/index/gilbert-tobin-sam-nickless>)</sup>.

## Safety and policy

- OpenAI committed $2 million to research on AI and children's safety, aimed at shared tools for measuring risks and opportunities for young people <sup>[6](<https://openai.com/index/children-first-declaration>)</sup>.
