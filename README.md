## AI models and LLM tooling

- OpenAI shipped GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, which Artificial Analysis scores at 62 on its Intelligence Index, just behind Fable 5.2 at 65, and Sol ties Astra at about 15% of the cost per task <sup>[1](<https://latent.space/p/ainews-gpt-6-2-astra-the-2-frontier-model-is-better-and-20-600-cheaper-but-has-mixed-reviews-6-2-sol/>)</sup>. Reviews are mixed, with some criticism of RL artefacts <sup>[1](<https://latent.space/p/ainews-gpt-6-2-astra-the-2-frontier-model-is-better-and-20-600-cheaper-but-has-mixed-reviews-6-2-sol/>)</sup>.

## Search, publishing and AI answers

- Google's AI Overviews appear on more than half the US keywords tracked by DataForSEO, up from 6% two years ago <sup>[2](<https://revscope.co/blog/search-seo-geo-2026/>)</sup>.
- In a randomized experiment, an AI Overview cut clicks to outside websites by about 40% <sup>[2](<https://revscope.co/blog/search-seo-geo-2026/>)</sup>.
- AI assistants sent websites just 0.3% of their traffic in March <sup>[2](<https://revscope.co/blog/search-seo-geo-2026/>)</sup>.
- A Berlin travel publisher reports losing 70% to 75% of its traffic to Google AI Overviews <sup>[3](<https://nicolasbouliane.com/blog/death-by-ai>)</sup>.

## Applied AI and emerging domains

- Osmo, an AI startup, says its foundation model for smell, trained on expert human sniffer annotations cross-referenced with chemical analysis, has reached "human-level olfactory intelligence" <sup>[4](<https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/18/1148816/smell-startup-osmo-ai-nose-good-as-humans/>)</sup>.

## Industry and partnerships

- OpenAI and Le Groupe Figaro partnered to bring Le Figaro journalism into ChatGPT through summaries and excerpts linking to original stories <sup>[5](<https://openai.com/index/le-groupe-figaro-and-openai-announce-partnership>)</sup>.
