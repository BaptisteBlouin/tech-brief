## AI Safety & Model Evaluation
- OpenAI introduces **Deployment Simulation**, a pre-release method to predict model behavior using realistic conversation contexts, expanding risk assessment and reducing evaluation awareness in candidate models <sup>[1](<https://openai.com/index/deployment-simulation>)</sup>.

## Enterprise AI & Strategy
- Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella emphasizes **Loopcraft**: a theory where "cognitive loops" between humans and AI systems create compounding "token capital," arguing that building learning loops atop models matters more than picking the best model <sup>[2](<https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-satya-on-loopcraft-building>)</sup>.

## AI Infrastructure & Hardware
- Google launches the **TPU Developer Hub**, a centralized resource with code-first guides, open-source recipes, and deep dives on TPU hardware/software optimization for training and inference workloads <sup>[3](<https://developers.googleblog.com/unlocking-the-power-of-the-tpu-stack-introducing-our-new-developer-hub/>)</sup>.
- UK grid operators test **AI-driven data center demand response**, dynamically throttling chip power to stabilize the grid during peak demand, demonstrating flexible, grid-aware compute <sup>[4](<https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/16/1138591/data-center-online-quickly-electric-grid-flex/>)</sup>.

## Data & Analytics Engineering

- dbt warns against **"lift-and-shift" migrations**, highlighting how blindly porting legacy workflows to dbt preserves old problems and undermines data trust <sup>[10](<https://www.getdbt.com/blog/start-fresh-don-t-lift-and-shift-a-dbt-migration-guide>)</sup>.

## Open-Source & Developer Tools
- **Datasette 1.0a34** adds in-UI row insert/edit/delete, inspired by Datasette Agent’s SQL write support, closing a long-standing gap in the web interface <sup>[11](<https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/16/datasette/#atom-everything>)</sup>.
- New **datasette-tailscale 0.1a0** plugin enables instant Tailscale-connected Datasette instances via a sidecar, using experimental `tailscale-rs` Python bindings <sup>[12](<https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/16/datasette-tailscale/#atom-everything>)</sup>.
- Georgi Gerganov (ggml) endorses **Qwen3.6-27B** as a capable local coding model, used daily for maintainer tasks on M2 Ultra/RTX 5090 with lightweight harnesses <sup>[13](<https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/16/georgi-gerganov/#atom-everything>)</sup>.

## Policy & Security
- Export controls on **Anthropic’s Claude Fable 5** are criticized as misguided: the "jailbreak" involved asking the model to "fix this code" on deliberately vulnerable samples, which security experts argue is expected and beneficial behavior for cyberdefense <sup>[14](<https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/16/fable-5-export-controls/#atom-everything>), [15](<https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jun/16/matteo-wong-the-atlantic/#atom-everything>)</sup>.
