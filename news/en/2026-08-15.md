## AI/LLM: Models, Tooling & Agents
- Agent frameworks are evolving rapidly: Vercel's *eve* and Fred Schott's *Flue* (now at v2, first stable) pioneer React-style "Agent Hooks," treating an agent as a JS function that re-renders on every turn to compose behavior, inspired by React's composability <sup>[1](<https://www.latent.space/p/flue-2>)</sup>.
- A perspective argues that AI's edge in complex tasks (e.g., mathematics) stems less from superior reasoning than from a vastly larger *external symbolic working memory* (context window) that retains problem statements, intermediate steps, constraints, and abandoned approaches simultaneously <sup>[2](<https://davidepiffer.com/p/ai-isnt-outthinking-mathematicians>)</sup>.
- Substack's new built-in AI detector spurs educational DIY: a tutorial shows how to build a simple AI text detector from scratch and use it as a verifier to train an SLM to evade detection, illustrating limitations and novel LLM applications beyond math/code reasoning <sup>[3](<https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/ai-detector-from-scratch>)</sup>.
- *ThoughtDAG* is an open demo of an editable context graph for LLM conversations, letting users visually manage and select which parts of long histories feed into the next request <sup>[4](<https://chenxiachan.github.io/thoughtdag/>)</sup>.

## AI Practice & Human Factors
- Working with AI feels more like leadership than coding: outcomes are less predictable, so context, clarity, feedback, and reusable instructions become more valuable than rigid prompts; treating AI as a collaborator improves results <sup>[5](<https://allen.bargi.org/notes/working-with-ai-feels-like-leadership/>)</sup>.

## Infrastructure & Hardware
- Google’s TPU (Tensor Processing Unit) is a custom AI accelerator designed for large-scale ML workloads <sup>[6](<https://blog.bytebytego.com/p/ep222-what-is-googles-tpu>)</sup>.

## DevOps & Cloud
- GitHub OAuth apps now support expiring access tokens (8-hour lifetime) and refresh tokens (6-month validity), enabling automatic token rotation; they also allow multiple redirect URIs and wildcard matching for both GitHub Apps and OAuth apps <sup>[7](<https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-14-multiple-redirect-uris-and-token-refresh-for-oauth-apps>)</sup>.
- A critical view argues Cloudflare has shifted from its "boring but reliable" infrastructure roots (DDoS protection, caching, DNS) toward a more marketing-driven, AI-centric posture that some long-time users find cringey, despite continued business success <sup>[8](<https://opensauce.it/cloudflare-ai-psychosis/>)</sup>.
