## MLOps, Data Engineering, and Infrastructure
- Google successfully reproduced Ai2's Olmo 3 7B pre-training from scratch using MaxText and JAX/XLA on Cloud TPUs, reaching up to 57.4% MFU while navigating cluster resizes <sup>[30](<https://developers.googleblog.com/reproducing-olmo-3-7b-pre-training-in-maxtext-case-study-of-large-scale-training-on-tpus/>), [31](<https://developers.googleblog.com/reproducing-olmo-3-7b-pre-training-in-maxtext-case-study-of-large-scale-training-on-tpus/>), [32](<https://developers.googleblog.com/reproducing-olmo-3-7b-pre-training-in-maxtext-case-study-of-large-scale-training-on-tpus/>)</sup>. - High-throughput inference tools emerged, such as **Quail**, combining a query planner and inference engine to exceed a billion tokens per minute on a single H100 GPU <sup>[35](<https://fsdatalab.github.io/blog/introducing-quail/>), [36](<https://modal.com/blog/quail-billion-tpm>)</sup>. In storage, Apache Parquet introduced **ALP**, an adaptive lossless floating-point encoding matching ZSTD ratios while decoding about 10x faster <sup>[37](<https://parquet.apache.org/blog/2026/09/22/alp-adaptive-lossless-floating-point-encoding-in-apache-parquet/>)</sup>. Turbopuffer v3 restructured its storage engine to separate full-text search, filters, and vector retrieval <sup>[38](<https://turbopuffer.com/blog/rip-vector-database>)</sup>.
- Infrastructure scale reached new heights with AMD acquiring World Labs for $8.2 billion <sup>[39](<https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-amd-buys-world-labs-for-82b>)</sup>, and Anthropic signing an $11.6 billion compute agreement with Akamai alongside a $60 billion Broadcom custom infrastructure deal <sup>[40](<https://www.ir.akamai.com/news-releases/news-release-details/akamai-announces-116-billion-multi-year-agreement-anthropic>), [41](<https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-02/broadcom-starts-amassing-60-billion-to-fund-chips-for-anthropic?accessToken=eyJhbGciOiJIUzI1NiIsInR5cCI6IkpXVCJ9.eyJzb3VyY2UiOiJTdWJzY3JpYmVyR2lmdGVkQXJ0aWNsZSIsImlhdCI6MTc5MDkyMjQzOCwiZXhwIjoxNzkxNTI3MjM4LCJhcnRpY2xlSWQiOiJUTTkwWUVLSzNOWUQwMCIsImJjb25uZWN0SWQiOiJFQTExNDNDNTM4NEE0RUY5QTg5RjJEN0IxMTg2MzcwOSJ9.ou2DaP8DyjOKlNzvvF7qkpSHPum6rWuWYguG_k-KLHo>)</sup>.
