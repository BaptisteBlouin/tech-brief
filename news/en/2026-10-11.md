- DuckDB detailed view-only database files storing no local data rows, acting as shareable browsers pointing directly to Parquet or object storage <sup>[70](<https://duckdb.org/2026/10/07/view-only-mode.html>)</sup>. A production AWS Bedrock guardrail pipeline reduced agent action latency from nearly 14 seconds to under 2 seconds by parallelizing independent safety layers and short-circuiting cheap rejection rules early <sup>[71](<https://techstrong.ai/contributed-content/why-your-ai-agent-pipeline-is-slow-and-how-to-fix-it-without-changing-models/>)</sup>.
